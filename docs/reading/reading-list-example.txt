THE TRAP — "MID AND HIGH RENDER AS SINGLE, IDENTICAL POINTS" IS FALSE, AND THE
CUBE SAYS SO

  Sprint 8's first done-when as written says the two upper panels render as
  single, identical points. Measured against the committed cube, they do not:

      Mid panel     16 dots spanning 0.1757 points   (32.3243 → 32.5000)
      High panel    16 dots spanning 0.0782 points   (32.4218 → 32.5000)
      Low panel     16 dots spanning 8.1346 points   (24.3654 → 32.5000)

  And Mid and High are not identical to each other. High's objective-5 column
  is dead flat at −0.0782 on every row; Mid's wobbles — −0.0782, −0.1757,
  −0.0537, −0.0799.

  THE CAUSE IS RESULTS 8.4 AND IT IS THE SOLVER, NOT THE MODEL. Objective 5 is
  `P(not running out)` over 2,000 paths, so it takes 2,001 values and its
  difference is zero across an INTERVAL rather than at a point. The bisection
  lands somewhere inside that plateau, about 0.08 points below `t_now`.
  Objectives 1, 3 and 4 are exactly `t_now` at all 32 upper-panel cells.

  WHY THIS IS A TRAP AND NOT A FOOTNOTE. If either upper panel auto-scales to
  its own data, 0.08 points becomes the full panel width and Mid and High will
  render with visible structure — four columns, one of them offset, looking
  exactly like a finding. Every number on screen correct. This is the third
  occurrence of the defect the two axis tests in `tests/test_app.py` were
  written for, and the first one where it would fabricate a lesson rather than
  delete one.

  DO NOT round the dots to hide it and DO NOT drop objective 5. Pin all three
  panels to one shared domain wide enough for Low, which makes the upper two
  collapse to a visual point, which is the truth. Then say so in the caption.
