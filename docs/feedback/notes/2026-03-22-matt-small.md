Thoughts:
1) Arrange the action order from left to right in the order you should be completing items or do a walk-through like Turbo-tax does.
2) If considering commercializing this, you might actually consider focusing this on recruiters or job boards instead of job hunters. Though that depends on what monetization approach you use.
3) For the "Analyze" it might be good to allow the user to choose the resume that is being considered. Though a broad "Have a discussion with the program about your job and typical activities" along with prompts would be very helpful too.
4) This popped up in the end of the Meta analysis: LinkedIn exclusion filter for recruiters/staffing firms encountered: James Search Group OR Hudson Modeling
5) After Analyzing, you can't click on the job descriptions to open them up. That would be very nice - particularly if you don't track what you pasted.
6) I'm assuming "Calibrate" and "Review & Enrich" are in progress.
7) For tailoring, you don't allow for tailoring of the jobs unless they're flagged as a good fit. I just think about the statistic that most women don't apply unless they hit 100% of the asks and guys are more like 70% or less. Applying for a moonshot isn't bad and, if you're going through a personal reference, you'll want the help tailoring your resume to be as good as possible.
8) Not sure how I feel about some of the formatting choices for the auto-generated resumes (e.g., bolding quantitative meterics). It also sounded very AI generated.
9) The order of positions on the resume weren't chronological. Looks like they were put in order of how many supporting words my resume descriptions had for that particular JD. That might not have been so bad, but it left in the years of employment, making it look like I had been unemployed for several years. I'm surprised that it didn't suggest removing dates to avoid that situation and concerns about age discrimination.
10) Apparently resume rewrites choke on CVs. The resume completely excluded any of the specialty things (e.g., patent agent certification) and publications that I had and/or any context that it might have derived from them. It'd have been cool if it also scraped those publications for things/context that could be used to enhance a resume. After all, PhDs do have publications (and many from industry have white papers or patents) that could greatly enhance a resume if incorporated correctly.
11) Note that some of the above may completely irrelevant depending on the situation. While highlighting high-value skills that are not directly called out in the listing may draw strong attention from a human, it will be ignored by AI filters. 
12) Hilarious note: Some other resume stuff was also jacked up. Most notably, it looks like it decided it didn't care where I was at for some of the jobs and just assigned what it considered reasonable. E.g., my pasted resume didn't include cities/states, but it looks like my LinkedIn profile got scraped to get them. However, it ignored some of that information. It decided that I was in Chandler when I worked at Intel and that my entire time with Healing Solutions was remote.
13) No way to remove old sessions.
14) On "Resumes" I see a "1/3" on the top right of the page... there's only the 1 page.
15) I like that you lock certain sections until they have the right information. But you might want to have a pop-up letting them know why they can't access it. Or, if you want to be cute, create a visual "ping" on the relevant section that must be completed. Be that a glowing/fading halo, text color flash/change, etc.
16) Instead of just pasting the text of the JD, a web page or a JD link would probably be better. Easier for the user and would allow you to link back to the actual posting (#5) or applying. It would also allow you to auto-populate the role and company information (able to be modified later).

Looks good/insanely helpful overall. Most of the comments are more on ways to get it closer to something akin to LinkedIn's "Easy Apply" by automatically tailoring your resume and allowing for simplified referencing to the job posting and application. Not much you can do about the resume stuff aside from tell people to review it because AI isn't perfect :/

Nice job!
