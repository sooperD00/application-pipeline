Job Titles to Test
Band A — LinkedIn-native, easy to export (your baseline "standard" resumes)
Software / IT engineer (skills-dense, project-heavy — your home turf, use as the control)
Registered nurse (large occupation and findable; licenses + clinical rotations + shift work make it a good license-parsing test)
Accountant / financial analyst (credential-forward: CPA, tidy linear arc)
Sales / account management (metric- and quota-laden language)
Marketing / comms (campaign framing, semi-portfolio)
Project / product manager (cross-functional achievement bullets)
Teacher / K-12 educator (certifications, but the format diverges sharply from corporate)
Band B — huge occupations, thin on LinkedIn (where onboarding must NOT break)
Retail / cashier (short, hourly, gaps, non-linear)
Food service — server, line cook, fast food (thin, sometimes no traditional resume at all)
Warehouse / logistics / commercial driver (CDL and safety certs, shift-based)
Customer service / call center
CNA / home health aide / caregiver (certifications + care experience, rarely bullet-quantified)
Administrative assistant / office clerk
Band C — structural edge cases worth hunting for deliberately (these are what actually snap parsers)
Skilled trades — electrician, plumber, HVAC (apprenticeship + license + union, no degree spine)
Career changer, incl. military-to-civilian (the arc is the whole challenge)
Freelance / creative / gig — designer, writer, contractor (portfolio-based, concurrent overlapping roles)
Academic / researcher (a CV, not a resume — pages long, publications; totally different object)
Senior executive, VP+ (length + seniority stress test)
New grad / entry-level (education-forward, internships, sparse)
